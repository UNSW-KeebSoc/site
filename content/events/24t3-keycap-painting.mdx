Keyboard looking a little dull?🫵 Need some colour in your life?🫵 Need to distract yourself from assignments?🫵 Well, come to ✨DrawSoc x KeebSoc's keycap painting event✨

Whether you want to paint your finest work on a **1u canvas or miniaturise your favourite memes,** we've got all the tools necessary for you to paint anything 🤩‼️

## What We Provide

-   Keycaps
-   Paint
-   _Note: No switches or keyboards will be included with the keycaps, however our switch tester keychains are available for purchase to accompany your painted keycap!_

This is the perfect opportunity to add some personality to your keyboard or create a unique art piece. No artistic experience needed - just bring your creativity!
