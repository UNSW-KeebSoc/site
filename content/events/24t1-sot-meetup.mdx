We will be hosting the first meetup of the year next week! 

Bring in your keebs to show to everyone! Don't have a keyboard? No worries, feel free to come and chill and look at some zesty keyboards! 

We will have:
- Switch testers to try out new switches! 🎮
- Workshop area with modding supplies (lube 205g0, dielectric grease, foam, etc.) 🛠️
- Prize giveaway (thank you to cheekywombatco for the artisan keycaps!) 🎉

What to Bring:
- Your favorite keyboard(s) to show off 🎨
- Any keyboards you want to work on modding 🔧
- Yourself and your enthusiasm for all things mechanical keyboards! 😄

Don't miss out on this great opportunity to meet fellow KeebSoc members, show off your boards, and potentially win some awesome prizes. See you there! 

*Thank you to everyone who signed up during O-Week! If you haven't already, please [sign up here](https://member.arc.unsw.edu.au/s/login/?ec=302&startURL=%2Fs%2Fclubdetail%3Fclubid%3D0016F000044IRD6QAO) to officially join the society.*
