## 🐈 Cat Photo Competition 🐈

✨ **KeebSoc** x **PCSoc** x **CatSoc** ✨ proudly present our purr-fect photo competition!

Pick the purr-fect photo of your beloved chonker (or purr-haps more) to submit to one of these three 🐈egories:

1) Cutest 🐈
2) Best Pose
3) Crustiest 🐈

And of course, there will be **bonus points** if there is a PC or Keyboard in the picture :3
